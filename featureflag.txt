### List Feature Flags
**URL:** https://api.shopsynch.com/v1/admin/feature-flags

**Type:** GET


**Content-Type:** application/x-www-form-urlencoded

**Description:** Returns feature flags for the admin dashboard. Optional filters are category,
         defaultStatus, and search.
